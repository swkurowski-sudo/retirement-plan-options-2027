What profit sharing does for the firm

Flexible every year

The amount is decided after year-end, up to the tax-return deadline. A lean year can mean zero; a strong year can mean the maximum.

Roughly triples attorney savings

Deferrals cap at about $24,500. Profit sharing adds up to about $47,500 more, for roughly $72,000 per attorney before catch-ups.

Saves tax at the firm level

Deductible to the S corp, which lowers the profit passed through to the attorneys. No payroll tax, unlike paying it as a bonus.

Most dollars go to the owners

A "new comparability" formula can give the attorneys the full amount while staff receive about 5% of pay. Each attorney's amount can differ.

Staff money can vest over time

Up to a 6-year schedule rewards staff who stay. Forfeitures offset future costs. Safe harbor money vests immediately.

Safe harbor can wait

The firm can add a 3% safe harbor contribution as late as about Dec 1, 2027 (4% until the end of 2028), after seeing what staff actually defer.

Sets up the cash balance plan

Cash balance plans pair with a 401(k) profit-sharing plan. Once added, attorney profit sharing is generally capped at 6% of pay.

Tax credits help pay for it

New-plan credits for 2027–2029, including up to $1,000 a year per staff member toward employer contributions.

  • Only W-2 salary counts, so low S corp salaries reduce what the attorneys can contribute.
  • Staff must receive a share.
  • The plan needs an annual fairness test and a Form 5500 filing, usually handled by the TPA.
